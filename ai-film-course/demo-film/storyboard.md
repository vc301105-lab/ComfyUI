## Assembly plan (Route A — the storyboard film)

1. Each frame cover-cropped to **1920×1080**, 24 fps.
2. Ken Burns: shots alternate slow push-in / pull-out (±~20% zoom over the shot).
3. 0.6 s crossfades between shots (padded overlay chain — see build log).
4. Title card **THE LAST LIGHTHOUSE** over shot 1 (1.0–5.5 s).
5. VO enters at 2.5 s, fades out at the end, loudness-normalized to −14 LUFS.
6. **Sound design** (auto-mixed when present): `audio/ambience.wav` (storm bed,
   ~18 dB under the VO) + `audio/music.wav` (score, ~25 dB under) — both
   synthesized in code: `audio/make_ambience.py`, `audio/make_music.py`.
7. End card: `WRITTEN, DIRECTED & GENERATED BY YOU` over shot 8's tail.
8. Export: `film.mp4` (1080p24, H.264, stereo AAC).

All of this is scripted in `build_film.sh` — run it to reproduce the film.

**9:16 vertical cut:** `build_vertical.sh` → `film-vertical.mp4` — same timeline,
but each frame becomes a slow horizontal pan of a 9:16 window (vertical crops
discard width, so pans — not zooms — are the right Ken Burns move).

**Thumbnail:** `make_thumbnail.py` → `thumbnail.png` (1280×720, poster + titles).
**Captions:** `make_subtitles.py` → `subtitles.srt`.

## Upgrade path to a full motion film

Replace each Ken Burns clip with an image-to-video take (start frame = this
frame, prompt = the motion column): Veo 3.1 / Runway Gen-4.5 / Kling 3.0 /
Luma Ray — or locally with the workflows in `models/workflows/`:
`wan-i2v-the-last-lighthouse.json` (Wan 2.6), `wan-firstlast-the-last-lighthouse.json`
(start+end frame — strongest consistency), `wan22-i2v-the-last-lighthouse.json` (Wan 2.2).
The full 3-layer sound design transfers as-is.

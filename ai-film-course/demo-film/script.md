# THE LAST LIGHTHOUSE — Script

**Logline:** When the oceans take the cities, the last lighthouse keeper on Earth keeps the lamp burning in case anyone is left to find their way home — and one morning, someone does.

**Format:** 60-second AI short film · 8 shots · voiceover only (no dialogue) · sci-fi / elegiac

**Feeling target:** loneliness that turns into hope — the viewer should get goosebumps when the ship appears.

---

## Narration (VO) — ~112 words / ~60 s

> I remember the day the oceans took the cities.
>
> I was sent to the last lighthouse on Earth — to keep the lamp burning, no matter what.
>
> The storms came harder every year. The radio fell silent long ago.
>
> But every night, I climbed the stairs and lit the lamp, and I told myself: if I keep the light alive... someone, somewhere, might find their way home.
>
> Then, one morning, a signal blinked on the horizon. A ship that should not exist.
>
> And for the first time in ten years... the lighthouse had someone to guide.

**Act map:** setup (lines 1–2) → struggle (lines 3–4) → turn (lines 5–6).

## Why this script is AI-friendly (the lesson)

- **One character, one location** → consistency is tractable.
- **VO, no lip-sync** → the hardest AI problem is avoided by design.
- **Every shot has one motion and one job** → clean image-to-video candidates.
- **The teal (storm) vs amber (lamp) palette** is baked into the story → a natural, unified grade.
